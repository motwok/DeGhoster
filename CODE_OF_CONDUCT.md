# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

DeGhoster is meant to be a friendly, useful project. Everyone who takes part —
reporting bugs, requesting features, discussing, or just using it — is welcome,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual orientation. We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give others the benefit of the doubt.
- Keep discussion focused on the topic and its technical merit.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and offer it kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks based on any of the characteristics listed
  above.
- **Radical and extremist ideologies — whether right-wing or left-wing — and anyone
  who advocates, condones, or is prepared to use violence have no place here.**
  Nazism in particular is named explicitly as **incompatible** with this project.
  Content that promotes, glorifies, or spreads such ideologies, and people who
  advocate them, are not welcome: such contributions and comments are removed and the
  account may be banned without further discussion.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Good faith and honest mistakes

This is a technical project, not an etiquette exam. **Honest mistakes made in good
faith are not violations** — for example getting someone's name or pronoun wrong, or a
simple misunderstanding. Just correct it, a brief apology if warranted, and move on.

Specifically on pronouns: **as long as someone's pronoun has not been clearly stated
or is not otherwise evident, using the one their presentation reasonably suggests is
not an offense** — and **"they/them" is always a safe, neutral default** when you're
unsure. In return, once someone has made their pronoun known, please simply use it;
insisting on the wrong one after being told is not a good-faith mistake.

What matters here is mutual respect and good faith, not perfect wording.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issue tracker, discussions,
commit and review comments, and any other project channel) and when an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
maintainer (**[@motwok](https://github.com/motwok)** on GitHub). Reports are handled
confidentially and in good faith.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and
may take any action deemed appropriate, from removing content or issuing a warning to
temporarily or permanently banning a participant. The maintainer has the final say.
